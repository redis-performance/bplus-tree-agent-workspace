## Ranking and dissent

All three rank exact16-byte native comparison first. ARM ranks scalar loop splitting
second and vector prefilter third; Intel ranks exact scalar masks second and scalar
unrolling/splitting third; AMD ranks exact scalar masks second and later decode/hash
third. All retain NONE as a valid fallback. Chair ranks native exact matching first:
strong directly mapped target, qualified controls, narrow helper-only scope and
existing correctness referee. Exact scalar masks rank second but evidence that their
extra arithmetic pays is weaker. Splitting/unrolling/prefilter rank third because
compiler specialization or repeated work weakens their premise. Later decode/hash
is outside the strongest attribution and increases semantic scope. No dissent on
the selected mechanism; proposals emphasize differing guard risks.

Novelty is implementation on this ARM source/host, not invention of SIMD or import
of x86 speed. Portability is conditional: original fallback on unsupported targets;
conservative little-endian AArch64+NEON guard unless lane mapping independently proven.
Dense matches and short leaves may lose; whole-TU placement can affect other operations.
