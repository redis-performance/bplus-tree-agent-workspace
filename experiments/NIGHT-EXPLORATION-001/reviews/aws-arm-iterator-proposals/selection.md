## Why select a bounded replication

The actual corrected native ARM source/compiler/consumer differs from the historical
Apple experiment, and both current50M score-only primary controls qualify. Actual
IteratorNext implements the width mask with MOV/CMP/LSL/CSINV followed by AND. The
retained A expression might shorten that dependency sequence under Clang18; whether
it does so and improves elapsed time are unanswered native questions. The baseline
already has no conditional branch in that mask sequence. No old timing, old compiler
codegen, AMD qualification or whole-decoder share substitutes for native evidence.

The reviewers' region counts are consistent: ARM excludes the final AND, which both
forms require, giving33 events and3.524%/3.666% sampled period. Intel/AMD include AND,
giving37 events and3.524%/4.421%. These are nested descriptive samples, not causal
costs, additive shares or a rigorous speed bound. A static reduction is only an
eligibility check; the fixed dual-primary comparison can still reject the hypothesis.

Ranked choices are (1) exact A replication with hard static/correctness/time stops,
(2) NONE if the concrete frozen plan fails review or cannot fit, (3) no other rewrite.
No B/C fallback, new caching state, dispatcher, cross-word change or renamed prior
hypothesis is selected. This chair judgment differs from two reviewers' preference
for NONE; their reasons remain substantive and do not get erased by a majority claim.
