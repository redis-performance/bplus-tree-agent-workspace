## Evidence and distinction

[The paid M6 map](../../020-member-index-profile-continuation/hot-region-map/MAP.md) identifies 184 of 417 SCAN PC locations inside the inline tag-match ranges. The actual scan-slot code has 514 instructions and scalar 64-bit SWAR tag matching, with no vector-register instructions. Complete-word loads, conservative masks, candidate-byte verification, folded-zero handling and output-position stores are visible. Exact vector comparisons could replace two word-mask constructions and their candidate verification with one bounded 16-byte exact comparison/mask reduction. This is a source mechanism with a directly observed region, not a claim that 184 samples measure removable cost. The 98 locations at slot+960 do not establish comparison latency, branch prediction failure or the source of any stall. Sampling/instrumentation/layout effects remain possible.

The [cross-host inventory](../../021-cross-host-scan-inventory/README.md) is strong contrary evidence: corrected ARM A/C regress roughly 70–100%, B roughly 55–78%, across both primary sizes. No repair or favorable interpretation of those results is proposed. Different compiler/native implementation could change that outcome; the frozen M6 scalar code alone does not predict it will. Corrected AMD SSE2 B also fails; its unchanged source on M6 would run fallback and cannot answer this native-NEON question.

This has not run on M6. It changes SCAN's **all-match enumeration**, not first-match `memchr` lookup, score decoding, rank sibling selection, update repacking, forced inlining, iterator caches, private bulk decoding or the closed 017 timing protocol. No completed M6 schedule is repeated. It introduces no early decode, prefetch, layout, allocator, hash or public semantic change.
