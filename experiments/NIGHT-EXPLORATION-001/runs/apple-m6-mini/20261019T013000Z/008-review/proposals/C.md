## Recommendation and evidence

Select **NONE** from my adversarial-causality lens. The new geometry establishes
that a potential independent score address exists; it does not establish that
issuing its read sooner is the remaining public-lookup bottleneck, or that a new
source-level intervention will benefit both declared primary sizes. I find no
other distinct supported mechanism within the frozen scanner/decoder/hash/layout
scope. This is not a vote to relabel the prior 100K wins or retry their patches.

The sufficient public-hit profiles put 57/139 PCs at100K and148/266 at1M in
platform memchr, with30/139 and27/266 respectively in the leaf helper. Those are
sampled regions, not exact cost shares. They do not identify which leaf load is
waiting, demonstrate a score-read cache miss, or establish an available latency
overlap interval. The small-miss captures are insufficient and should remain so
rather than being repeated unchanged.

The new census makes an important distinction: score-word/member-record starts
occupy different virtual64-byte diagnostic blocks for all observed nonzero-width
candidates and matches, while tag/score starts also differ. It does **not** observe
hardware cache-line residency, misses, memory-level parallelism or dependency
stall time. Both addresses could already be available cheaply. Existing release
assembly shows record validation before the final score decode, but instruction
order and address separation alone do not establish the time spent on that chain.

Each successful public query produces roughly1.14–1.15 tag candidates. That modest
false-candidate rate makes prospective score work conceivable; it does not make it
free. At321bytes, almost all extra tag candidates are rejected by the existing
external full-hash check before memcmp. Score work performed before that rejection
would introduce traffic into an otherwise successful cheap filter. Misses rarely
enter a leaf at all, so their favorable cost cannot be inferred from hit geometry.

Ordinary fixtures contain no reversed leaves and overwhelmingly6/7-bit deltas.
Therefore an early score load/prefetch or new address-generation arrangement could
improve the ordinary case while losing on width0, wide finite scores, ninth-byte
spills, reversed orientation, null score output or external members. The existing
exact-score oracle establishes semantics for old patches, not throughput safety
for a new score-read hypothesis.

Finally,006 already produced the same normalized ScoreRaw instruction stream for

using source shape or a favorable median as proof of a useful new mechanism. It
does not rule out all future lookup improvements; it means old source rearrangement
and the new geometric observation must not be combined into an unsupported causal

## Revisit condition

Reopen a proposal when a distinct portable intervention has a discriminating
prediction grounded in the observed path. For example, a score-address scheduling
idea needs a preregistered locality/working-set or dependency observation that can
distinguish benefit from extra traffic and generated-code placement. PMU availability
is not mandatory: a bounded public-API fixture diagnostic and matched assembly/profile
can also supply useful evidence. The diagnostic must preserve the original lookup
work, use new frozen evidence paths, and avoid treating its instrumented times as
baseline speed or repeating the closed failed schedules.

Before any score-read implementation, freeze explicit public throughput guards for
reversed leaves and broad exact finite scores, including zero/full/spill widths,
signed zeros and both nullable outputs. Construction histories must actually
produce those properties; a source-included private oracle alone is insufficient.
These augment every101 existing case and both100K/1M primaries. They cannot replace
ordinary, long/binary-key, miss/mixed, rank, mutation, batch, SCAN/resize or owned-memory
guards. No tree-size eligibility switch is justified by these inputs.
