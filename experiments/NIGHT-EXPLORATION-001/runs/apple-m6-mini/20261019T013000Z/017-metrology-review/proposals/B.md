## Why counterbalancing alone is not an eligible new protocol

012 already uses same-seed serial ABBA. That balances a linear change across four
equally spaced windows: the two labels have the same mean position. It does not
balance nonlinear position effects or effects of the preceding context. This is a
mathematical limitation of the schedule, **not a diagnosis of 012**.

Complementing ABBA with BAAB could reveal whether label effects reverse with
position. However, averaging opposite effects into an eight-window estimate could
make a zero-effect baseline look precise while each original four-window control
still fails±1%. Treating that cancellation as qualification would weaken the
every-block gate. Requiring each four-window control to pass avoids that error,
but the frozen evidence supplies no mechanism by which simply relabeling or
reordering those windows would make the individual controls stable. I therefore
do not select a counterbalance-only retry.

Likewise, reducing context changes through a persistent process or shared fixture
would create a new execution context. A/A in that context could remove launch,
allocation or placement variation without showing that two different candidate
implementations have comparable placement and preceding-work effects. Historical
placement/caller/transport failures cannot be bypassed by assuming that transfer.
I have no new controlled placement/context intervention supported by the common
inputs, so I do not propose another such implementation under a new label.

## What a later qualifying design would have to establish

Qualification must retain all eight contracts at 100K/1M, fixed seeds/counts and
all rows, with every original block inside±1%, each window at least 10 ms, exact
counts/checksums/fixture fingerprints, and unchanged tree-owned memory. CPU/QoS
observations can describe context, never select or discard rows.

A genuinely new method would also need explicit controls for implementation
placement and preceding context, carried unchanged into candidate ABBA. Baseline
A/A would have to use the same independently addressable execution slots and
fixture ownership as that comparison. Any slot/link-order or context crossover
must be preregistered and qualify separately rather than be averaged to conceal
its failure. Correctness and ownership checks must cover the changed harness
lifecycle; qualified timing would still require fresh source-specific profiling,
the 2% speed gate, guard/memory gates and independent confirmation. Baseline
repeatability alone cannot establish those candidate properties.

These are requirements for reconsideration, not a proposed combined protocol or
authorization to implement one. A distinct controllable factor and evidence that
its controls can transfer are missing here.

## Budget and failure decision

No pilot is selected: 0 timed seconds and 0 measurement processes. No cells, seeds,
counts or thresholds are changed, and no failed historical results are replaced.
I cannot justify spending the ≤5 minute timed/15 minute wall pilot allowance on an
unchanged method or on cancellation that does not satisfy the existing block gate.
The ~45 minute preparation/review/pilot envelope and 07:15UTC total cutoff do not
remove the subsequent need for transferable controls and independent confirmation.
