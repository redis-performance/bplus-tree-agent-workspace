## One falsifiable protocol hypothesis

Keeping each label's process and read-only fixture alive across its two positions within a paired quartet, while blocking the unused worker, will make corrected-baseline A/A qualify in every original iterator cell and in both predeclared code-placement contexts. The counterfactual remains fresh process/fixture per position. Work per timed window, clocks and precision gates stay fixed. This tests the complete process/fixture-lifetime policy, including unavoidable changes in scheduling and state continuity; it does not isolate allocation, ASLR, cache, frequency or migration.

The new variable is lifetime across the four positions, rather than duration, warmup amount, QoS, caller specialization, idle delay, parent bookkeeping or completion transport. Historical DIAG010–018 tried those other variables, including placement alone, predecessor processes and transport, without a qualified adoption. Retaining an already constructed tree in each of two blocked workers is not the same as changing the coordinator's post-exit delay: the worker does not exit between its labelled observations. Placement here is a mandatory stress control; no padding value is selected for speed.
